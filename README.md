One-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026: 42 verified stories across 7 regions, three lead stories with drawn scenes, twelve illustrated briefs, a UTC timeline, a stylised world map with 12 numbered locations, a DATE CHECK box and Monday's market closes. Built to read at thumbnail, half and full size.

Entry for [Sum up 24 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124) on make.fun.

## Run

```sh
python3 render.py
python3 main.py
```

`render.py` draws `news.png` (1024x1024, Pillow only). `main.py` copies it to `/output/entry.png`. No network, deterministic.

- Masthead: Tuesday, 24 November 2026 — 42 stories, 7 regions, 13 fields.
